Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is The Lunch Desk?

The Lunch Desk is an office lunch coordination service that helps businesses organize and place group food orders from local restaurants.

Do you deliver the food?

The Lunch Desk primarily coordinates orders and restaurant pickup arrangements. Delivery availability may depend on the restaurant or specific order.

How much does it cost?

Pricing may vary depending on the order and services required. Contact The Lunch Desk with your lunch details for more information.

Can you help with recurring office lunches?

Yes. The Lunch Desk can help businesses organize recurring weekly, monthly, or scheduled office meals.

Can employees order different meals?

Depending on the restaurant and size of the order, The Lunch Desk can help coordinate individual meal selections or group-style meals.

Do you work with restaurants?

Yes. Local restaurants interested in receiving additional business and group orders can contact The Lunch Desk about becoming a restaurant partner.

What areas do you serve?

The Lunch Desk coordinates office lunches for businesses throughout Myrtle Beach, Conway, and the greater Horry County, South Carolina area.

How far in advance should I submit a request?

The sooner the better — more notice usually means more restaurant options, especially for large groups. When you send your request, we'll confirm what's possible for your date and let you know if a restaurant needs extra lead time.

Is there a minimum or maximum group size?

The Lunch Desk can help with everything from a small team lunch to a large all-office order. Minimums, when they apply, depend on the individual restaurant, and we'll confirm those details before anything is placed.

How do we get started?

Send your lunch details through the Plan Your Lunch form or reach out directly. We'll follow up to confirm availability, share options that fit your budget, and coordinate the order.
